# BeeHive design direction

Paper #f8f7f2; ink #20271f; honey #edb43b; meadow #6b8463; mist #e8e9e1; warning #bc443b.
Manrope for expressive, tightly spaced headings; DM Sans for readable working interfaces.

The hero is an asymmetric field station: a large left-aligned statement, beside a custom vector apiary floating above topographic terrain. Actual telemetry and proof packets connect physical honey to digital evidence. Its amber honey chamber is the single focal point. Quiet navigation and generous margins frame it.

The traceability sequence is a dark instrument panel with seven sequential stations. Dashboard surfaces use paper, compact metadata and distinct chart regions. Public verification resembles a provenance label with a clear verdict and expandable technical evidence. Use green exclusively for checked health/integrity, red for mismatches.

Reviewed against brief: avoid identical card grids and unnecessary WebGL. Nature appears through hive geometry, contour lines and apiary locations. Animation principles from local Three.js skill (one clock, bounded active effects, pause offscreen) are translated to the required Framer Motion and SVG runtime. No Three.js bundle is needed.
